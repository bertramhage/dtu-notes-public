## Definition
- $f(n)=O(g(n))$ if exists constants $c,n_0>0$, such that for all $n≥n_0$, $f(n)≤cg(n)$
- $f(n)=\Omega(g(n))$ if exists constants $c, n_0>0$, such that for all $n≥n_0$, $f(n)≥cg(n)$
- $f(n)=\Theta(g(n))$ if $f(n)=O(g(n))$ and $f(n)=\Omega(g(n))$
