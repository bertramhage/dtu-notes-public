## Single artificial neuron
![[Pasted image 20250901134105.png|500]]
**Input**: $x$, **Weight**: $w$, **Bias**: $b$

**Preactivation:** $z=wx+b$
**Activation**: $h=g(z)$
where $\sigma$ is a non-linear activation function

## Network of neurons
![[Pasted image 20250901134300.png]]

**Input**: $\mathbf{x}$
**Preactivation**: $W\mathbf{x}+b$
**Activation**: $\mathbf{h}=\sigma(z)$

Where
$$W=\begin{bmatrix}w_{11} & w_{12} & \dots & w_{1D}\\
w_{21} & w_{22} & \dots & w_{2D}
\end{bmatrix}$$
and $\sigma$ is applied elementwise


## Shallow neural network
Neural network with **1 hidden layer**.

$$
f_{\phi}(\mathbf{x})=\sigma ^{(2)}(W^{(2)}\sigma ^{(1)}(W^{(1)}\mathbf{x}+b^{(1)})+b^{(2)})
$$
$\mathbf{h}=\sigma ^{(1)}(W^{(1)}\mathbf{x}+b^{(1)})$,  $\mathbf{y}=\sigma ^{(2)}(W^{(2)}\mathbf{h}+b^{(2)})$

## Activation functions

**For hidden layers**:
- Sigmoid: $\sigma(z)=\frac{1}{1+e^z}$
- Arc-tangent: $\sigma(z)=atan(z)$
- Hyperbolic tangent: $\sigma(z)=\tanh(z)$
- Rectified linear: $\sigma(z)=max(0,z)$
![[Pasted image 20250901135552.png|600]]

**For output layers**:
- Regression: identity: $\sigma(z)=z$
- Classification: softmax $\sigma(z_i)=\frac{e^zi}{\sum_ie^zi}$
