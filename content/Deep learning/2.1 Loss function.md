For training data $\left\{ (x_i, y_i) \right\}_i$ we want to find neural net parameters
$$
\phi=\left\{ w^{(l)}, b^{(l)} \right\}_l
$$
s.t. we minimize the mismatch between $f_{\phi}(x_i)$ and $y_i$.

We define a loss function capturing this mismatch
$$
\hat{\phi}=\arg \min_\phi L(\phi).
$$

## Maximum likelihood estimation
Find parameters that maximises the probability of the data $\left\{ (x_i, y_i) \right\}_i$
$$
\hat{\phi}=\arg\max_\phi \prod_ip(y_i|x_i,\phi)
$$
$$
\hat{\phi}=\arg\max_\phi \sum_i\log p(y_i|f_{\phi}(x_i))
$$
$$
L(\phi) = -\sum_i\log p(y_i|f_{\phi}(x_i))
$$
$$
\hat{\phi}=\arg\min_\phi L(\phi)
$$
## Probabilistic inference
Normally use the most probable value:
$$
\hat{y}=\arg \max_y p(y|f_{\phi}(x))
$$
### Gaussian distribution (regression)
We assume $y$ is Gaussian distributed.
The loss (negative log-likelihood) becomes
$$
L(\phi)=-\sum_i\log p(y_i|f_\phi(x),\sigma)
$$
$$
=-\sum_i \left( -\frac{(y_i-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}-\frac{1}{2}\log 2\pi \sigma^2 \right) 
$$
Assuming $mu=f_{\phi}(x)$ we have
$$
\hat{\phi}=\arg \min L(\phi)=\sum_i(y_i-f_{\phi}(x))^2
$$

### Categorical distribution (multiclass classification)
If $y$ is categorical and is one-hot encoded then
$$
p(y|\pi)=\prod_d\pi ^{y_d}_d
$$
where $\pi=f_\phi(x)$

The loss (negative log-likelihood) becomes
$$
L(\phi)=-\sum_i\sum_d y_{id}\log \pi_d
$$
**Softmax activation function**:
Convert neural network outputs into probabilities
$$
\pi_d=\frac{\exp(z_d)}{\sum_d \exp(z_d)}
$$
