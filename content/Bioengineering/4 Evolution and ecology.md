8.7M eukaryotic species on earth (1.2M known to science)

## Mechanisms of evolution
1. Natural variation
2. Inheritance
3. Selection

### Sources of generic variability
DNA can change by
- Mutation
- Recombination
- Horizontal transfer

## Viruses
- Group 1: Double-stranded DNA (eg. herpes)
- Group 2: Single stranded DNA
- Group 3: Double-stranded **RNA**
- Group 4: Positive sense single-stranded RNA (eg. covid)
- Group 5: Negative sense single-stranded RNA (eg. Influenza)
- Group 6: Single-stranded RNA with a reverse transcriptase (eg. HIV)
- Group 7: Double-stranded RNA with a reverse transcriptase (eg. Hepatitis B)
