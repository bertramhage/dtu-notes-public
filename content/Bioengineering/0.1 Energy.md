## Thermodynamics laws
1. Energy can't neither be created or destroyed, only transformed
2. Entropy tends to increase over time (decreasing the amount of energy available to do work)

## Entropy
Defined as
$$
\frac{\text{amount of heat exchanged}}{\text{temperature at which exchange}}=\frac{\Delta H}{T}
$$
### Free energy
Free energy **$G$** is
$$
G=H-TS
$$
