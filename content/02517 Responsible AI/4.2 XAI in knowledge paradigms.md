## Schools of thoughts in XAI
- "Do an explanation even exist?" - Some believe an explanation might not always exist
- "How do we find the explanation? Do we have access to learning it?"

## Knowledge taxonomy
### Ontology:
- A study of the existence of things
- For explanations: "Do an explanation exist?"

**Realists**: An explanation *do* exist even though we do not know it
**Idealist**: Reject the existence of an explanation outside a human 

### Epistemology
- The study of knowledge: How do we obtain it
- For explanations: Weather and how much we are able to learn about the explanations

**Positivists**: Full comprehension of reality is possible
**Structuralists**: Explanations are only understandable from a standpoint (eg. a scientific field)
**Constructivists**: Reality can only be understood through human mental constructions, opinions, and preferences: "A good explanation is one that has the most conformity with human understanding"
**Relationists**: We can only understand explanations in relation to other explanations

## **Epistemological Perspectives on AI Explanations**
### Logical positivists
**Problematic**: Complete description: The explanation definition is complete
**Methodology**: Directly from description: the method is free of assumptions and simplifications
**Evaluation**: The method does not rely on results for justifying the explanations

### Contemporary realists
**Problematic**: Subjective description – Description is with respect to a narrow or limited view point
**Methodology**: Subjective development – The explanation is buildt to solve a proxy task
**Evaluation**: Pragmatists (optimizing an objective): The explanation is tested w.r.t. a task

### Interpretivist
**Problematic**: Human-idealised description – Explanation defined by inter subjective expectations
**Methodology**: Follow human understanding – AI explanations mimic humans'
**Evaluation**: Ranking on human data

### Postmodernist
**Problematic**: Rejection or ignorance of explanations – Not considering explicit explanation of individual model decisions
**Methodology**: Based on *comparison* and differences – Explanation is built by making a comparison of multiple inputs
**Evaluation**: Neopragmatists (conformity with human intentions) – The explanation helps in making/changing eternal properties

## Taxonomy classification tree
1. Does it explain the decision of more than one inference step of the model? => Postmodern
2. No: Does it include all the model creation steps? => Logical positivists
3. No: Does try to mimic human interpretation? => Interpretative
4. No: Is it based on specific questions or goals? => Contemporary positivitst
5. No => Postmodern

## Limitations
As in epistemology
- Assumption that the world remains as is
	- XAI can be justified in other domains later
	- Multi-paradigmatic approaches
- Epistemic justifications is subject to nroms
