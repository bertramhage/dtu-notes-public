Influence(Mises,$x$)=$log(p(\text{Mises}|\text{sentence}))-log(p(\text{Mises}|\text{sentence}-{x}))$

>[!info] Mutual information
>"How many bits of $X$ can we obtain by knowing $Y$?"
>$$
>I(X,Y)=\sum_{x \in X, y \in Y}\log_2 \frac{p(x,y)}{p(x)p(y)}  \, 
>$$
>
>**Point wise mutual information**
>"How many bits of $x$ can we obtain by knowing $y$?"
>$$
>pmi(x,y)=\log_2 \frac{p(x,y)}{p(x)p(y)}
>$$
