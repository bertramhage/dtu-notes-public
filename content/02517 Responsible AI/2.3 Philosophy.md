## Randomness
- Used for _Efficiency_ (SGD), _Creativity_ (Hypothesis generation), and _Equality_.
- **Note:** Randomness provides equality (blind chance) but not necessarily equity (fairness).
- **Justice as Fairness**: eg. "I will grade all students by random, thus it's fair"

## Human vs. Machine Ethics
- **Objectification:** The risk of treating humans as "subjects of control" to be managed/cured/trained.
- **Reductionism:** Reducing human "will" to simple brain interactions.
- **Agency:** AI cannot "will"; it can only calculate or randomise. Humans use "will" to break ties when logic fails.
