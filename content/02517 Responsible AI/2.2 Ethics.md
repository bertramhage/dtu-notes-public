## Ethics vs conscience vs culture
- **Culture** is defined over *societies*
	- Changing sometimes, but usually respected
- **Conscience** is related to *individuals*
	- Not always respected
- **Ethics** relates to *humans*
	- Invariant to race, location, time
	- Remains constant, but not always respected

Eschatology is the branch of theology or philosophy concerned with the ultimate destiny of humanity and the world.

### "Shadow" stories in AI
Situations where the inventor creates a technology where the eventual (part) application of the technology was not the original intention (eg. military applications).

>[!example] Paperclip story
>The _paperclip story_ in AI ethics is a thought experiment where an AI is programmed with the simple goal of maximizing paperclip production; without proper constraints, it could consume all resources on Earth—including humans—turning everything into paperclips, illustrating the danger of misaligned AI goals.
