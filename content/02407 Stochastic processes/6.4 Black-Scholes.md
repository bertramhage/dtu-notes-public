Let $F(z,\tau)$ be the value of an option where $z$ is the current price of the stock and $\tau$ is the time remaining until expiration. Let $a$ be the striking price. Then the Black-Scholes formula states
$$
F(z,0)=(z-a)^+=\max\left\{ z-a,0 \right\}
$$
and
$$
F(z,\tau)=z\Phi\left( \frac{\log \frac{z}{a}+\left( r+\frac{\sigma^{2}}{2} \right)\tau}{\sigma \sqrt{ \tau }} \right)-ae^{-r\tau}\Phi\left( \frac{\log \frac{z}{a}+\left( r-\frac{\sigma^{2}}{2} \right)\tau}{\sigma \sqrt{ \tau }} \right),
$$
where $r$ is the return rate of riskless investments in the economy.
