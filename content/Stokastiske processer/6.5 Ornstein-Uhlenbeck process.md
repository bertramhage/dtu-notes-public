The Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process $\left\{ V(t),t≥0 \right\}$ has two parameters, a drift coefficient $\beta>0$ and a diffusion parameter $\sigma^{2}$. As starting $V(0)=v$ it is defined
$$
V(t)=ve^{-\beta t}+\frac{\sigma e^{-\beta t}}{\sqrt{ 2\beta }}B(e^{2\beta t}-1),\quad \text{ for }t≥0,
$$
where $B(t)$ is a standard Brownian motion.

Mean and variance is given by
$$
E[V(t)]=ve^{-\beta t},\quad Var[V(t)]=\sigma^{2}\left( \frac{1-e^{-2\beta t}}{2\beta} \right)
$$
and covariance
$$
Cov[V(u), V(s)]=\frac{\sigma^{2}}{2\beta}(e^{-\beta(s-u)}-e^{-\beta(s+u)}),\quad 0<u<s.
$$
#### Cumulative density function
Using the knowledge of the mean and variance we can write the c.d.f. in terms of the standard normal distribution
$$
Pr\left\{ V(t)≤y|V(0)=x \right\} =\Phi\left( \frac{\sqrt{ 2\beta }(y-xe^{-\beta t})}{\sigma \sqrt{ 1-e^{-2\beta t} }} \right).
$$
## Infinitessimal definition of Ornstein-Uhlenbeck
Considering a change in $V$, $\Delta V$, as an effect of a change in $t$, $\Delta t$ we get
$$
E[\Delta V|V(t)=v]=-\beta v\Delta t+o(\Delta t),\quad Var[\Delta V|V(t)=v]=\sigma^{2}\Delta t+o(\Delta t)
$$

## Integrated Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process (position process)
If $V(t)$ is the velocity of a particle, then its position at time $t$ would be
$$
S(t)=S(0)+\int_{0}^{t} V(u) \, du ,
$$
where $t≥0$ and $\left\{ V(t),t≥0 \right\}$ is an Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process.

It follows
$$
E[S(t)]=0,\quad Var[S(t)]=\frac{\sigma^{2}}{\beta^{2}}\left( t-\frac{2}{\beta}(1-e^{-\beta t})+\frac{1}{2\beta}(1-e^{-2\beta t}) \right),
$$
with $S(0)=V(0)=0$.

## Stationary version of Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process
Considering the limiting distribution of the Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process
$$
\lim_{ t \to \infty } Pr\left\{ V(t)<y|V(0)=x \right\}=\Phi\left( \frac{\sqrt{ 2\beta }y}{\sigma} \right)
$$
the stationary Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process $\left\{ V^s(t),-\infty<t<\infty \right\}$ is represented in terms of a Brownian motion by
$$
V^s(t)=\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{ 2\beta }}e^{-\beta t}B(e^{2\beta t}),\quad -\infty<t<\infty.
$$
The covariance function for a stationary Ornstein-Uhlenbeck process is given by
$$
Cov[V^s(s),V^s(t)]=\frac{\sigma^{2}}{2\beta}e^{-\beta|t-s|}.
$$
