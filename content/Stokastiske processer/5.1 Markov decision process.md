>[!info] Definition and notation
>A Markov Decision Problem (MDP) is a process of 
>$$
>(\mathcal{T}, \mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}_\mathcal{S}, p, c),
>$$
>where ($\times$ denotes the Cartesian-product)
>+ $\mathcal{T}$ is a set of decision epochs, eg.
>	+ $\mathcal{T}=\mathbb{R}$, $\mathcal{T}=\mathbb{N}_0$, $\mathcal{T}=\left\{ 0,\dots,N \right\}$.
>+ $\mathcal{S}$ is the state space, eg.
>	+ $\mathcal{S}=\mathbb{R}^n$, $\mathcal{S}=\left\{ 0,\dots,N \right\}$.
>+ $\mathcal{A_S}$ is the action space,
>	+ eg. $\mathcal{A_s}=\left\{ 0,1 \right\}^n$, $\mathcal{A_s}=\left\{ 0,\dots,N-s \right\}$, $s\in\mathcal{S}$.
>	+ Policies are automated decisions
>		+ $\pi:\mathcal{S}\times \mathcal{T}\to \mathcal{A},  \pi(s,i)=a\in\mathcal{A}$, or
>		+ $\pi:\mathcal{S}\to \mathcal{A},  \pi(s)=a\in\mathcal{A}$ for the *stationary* case.
>+ $p(\cdot|s,a)$ are the transition probabilities, where
>	+ the controller's interactions are (usually) probabilistic:
>		+ $p(\cdot|s,a):\mathcal{S}\to[0,1]$.
>	+ Stationary policies induce a DTMC:
>		+ $P_{s,t}=p(t|s,\pi(s))$.
>+ $c(s,a,t)$ is the cost function:
>	+ $c:\mathcal{S}\times \mathcal{A_S}\times \mathcal{S}\to \mathbb{R}$, $c(s,a,t)\in\mathbb{R}$.
>	+ The expected costs:
>		+ $c(s,a)=E[c(s,a,T)]=\sum_{t\in\mathcal{S}}p(t|a,s)\cdot c(s,a,t)  \,$,
>	+ under a given policy is
>		+ $c_i^\pi(s)=c(s,\pi(s,i))=E[c(s,\pi(s,i), T)]$.

## Optimizing over a finite horizon
We pose the objective function
$$
v_{\gamma}^\pi(s_0)=E\left[ \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} \gamma^ic(S_i,\pi(S_i,i), S_{i+1})+\gamma^N\sigma(S_N)|S_0=s_0 \,  \right],
$$
where $\gamma\in\mathbb{R}$ is the discount factor ($0<\gamma<1$), and $\sigma:\mathcal{S}\to \mathbb{R}$ is the scrap cost or salvage value (last reward).

We use the Backward Induction algorithm, which initializes
$$
v_{\gamma}^*(s,0)=\sigma(s),
$$
and proceeds by recursion
$$
v_{\gamma}^*(s,i)=\min_{a\in\mathcal{A_s}}\left\{ c(s,a)+\gamma \sum_{t\in S}p(t|s,a)\cdot v_{\gamma}^*(t,i-1) \right\}.
$$
The optimal policy is thus
$$
\pi_{\gamma}^*(s,i)=\underset{a\in\mathcal{A_s}}{\arg\min}\left\{ c(s,a)+\gamma \sum_{t\in S}p(t|s,a)\cdot v_{\gamma}^*(t,i-1) \right\}.
$$
## Optimizing over an infinite horizon
For an infinite horizon we consider the objective function
$$
v_{\gamma}^\pi(s_0)=E\left[ \sum_{i=0}^{\infty} \gamma^ic(S_i,\pi(S_i), S_{i+1})|S_0=s_0 \,  \right],
$$
again, with discount factor $\gamma\in\mathbb{R}$ $(0<\gamma<1)$.

The convergence of a solution can be found utilizing the Bellman operator
$$
Lv(s)=\min_{a\in\mathcal{A_s}}\left\{ c(s,a)+\gamma \sum_{t\in S}p(t|s,a)\cdot v(t) \right\} 
$$
with infinity norm
$$
\mid\mid v\mid\mid_{\infty}=\max_{s\in \mathcal{S}}|v(s)|
$$
which is a contraction mapping
$$
\mid\mid Lu-Lv\mid\mid_\infty≤\gamma\mid\mid u-v\mid\mid_\infty
$$
