## DNA replication
Requires:
- DNA *template*
- 4 deoxyribonucleotide triphosphates (dATP, dGTP, dCTP, dTTP) building blocks
- DNA polymerase (puts it together)
- Primase (starter)

## DNA Transcription (RNA synthesis)
Requires:
- DNA *template*
- Building blocks (ATP, GTP, CTP, UTP)
- RNA polymerase (enzyme)

Initiation -> elongation -> termination

## Protein translation
RNA alphabet: A, C, G, U
