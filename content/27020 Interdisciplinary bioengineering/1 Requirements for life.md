## Characteristics
- Appear to violate second law of thermo dynamics (but of course don't)
- Evolution from simple to complex
- Cellular organisations

### Energy that life can harvest
- Light
- Inorganic compounds
- Organic compounds

### Energy stores
- Salt ions, protons
- fat, starch
- ATP (short lived)

## Requirements for life
- Liquid water
- An energy source
- Temperatures compatible with biomolecules

Nice to have:
- Magnetic field and dense atmosphere to protect from radiation
- A large moon
- Gas planets to protect from comets, etc

"Goldilocks" zone

### Water
Is good because it is polar

## Physical contraints on life
- Gravitational force
- Diffusion of substances or heat
- Limited available nergy
- Fluid transport

## Transport of substances through diffusion
Change in concentration field becomes (Fick's law):
$$
j=-D \frac{\partial c}{\partial x}
$$
D is diffusion coefficient
