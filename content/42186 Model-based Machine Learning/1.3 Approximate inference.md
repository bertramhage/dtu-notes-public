## Approximate inference
For most problems of interest, it is often infeasible to evaluate posterior exactly or to compute expectations with respect to it.

### Stochastic approximation
Use samples to compute expectations w.r.t. the posterior.
Example: Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC)

![[Pasted image 20260225132316.png|300]]
### Variational approximation
Approximate intractable distribution with a simpler, tractable one.
Goal: Find the parameters of the **simpler distribution** (eg. Gaussian) that make it **as similar as possible** (eg. using KL-divergence) to the true distribution.
