## Probabilistic PCA
For observed variable vector $\mathbf{x}_n \in \mathbb{R}^D$ introduce an explicit latent variable $\mathbf{z}_n \in \mathbb{R}^K$ where $K\ll D$.

Define a Gaussian prior distribution $p(\mathbf{z}_n)$ over the latent variable
$$
p(\mathbf{z}_n)=\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{z}_n|\mathbf{0,I})
$$

Define Gaussian conditional distribution
$$
p(\mathbf{x}_n|\mathbf{z}_n)=\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{x}|\mathbf{W}\mathbf{z}_n+\mathbf{\mu}, \sigma^{2}\mathbf{I})
$$

**Generative process**
1. For each observation $n\in\{ 1,\dots N \}$
	1. Sample latent projection $\mathbf{z}_n\sim \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{z}_n|\mathbf{0,I})$
	2. Sample observation $\mathbf{x}_n\sim \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{x}_n|\mathbf{Wz}_n+\mathbf{\mu}, \sigma^{2}+\mathbf{I})$

We can compute the marginal likelihood of the observations $\mathbf{x}_n$:
$$
\begin{aligned}
p(\mathbf{x}_n) &= \int p(\mathbf{x}_n|\mathbf{z}_n) p(\mathbf{z}_n) d\mathbf{z}_n \\
&= \int \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{x}_n|\mathbf{W}\mathbf{z}_n + \boldsymbol{\mu}, \sigma^2\mathbf{I}) \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{z}_n|\mathbf{0}, \mathbf{I}) d\mathbf{z}_n \\
&= \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{x}_n|\boldsymbol{\mu}, \mathbf{WW}^T + \sigma^2\mathbf{I})
\end{aligned}
$$
If we standardize the observation $\mathbf{x}_n$ we can set $\mathbf{\mu}\triangleq 0$

We find maximum likelihood as
$$
\mathbf{W}_{\text{MLE}}, \sigma_{\text{MLE}}=\operatorname*{arg\,max}_{\mathbf{W}, \sigma}\sum_{n=1}^{N} p(\mathbf{x}_n) \, 
$$

### Advantages of PPCA
- **Handling Missing Data:** Because it is a probabilistic model, it can naturally "fill in" missing values based on the learned distribution.  
- **Model Selection:** The use of a **likelihood function** allows you to mathematically compare PPCA to other models and automatically determine the best number of dimensions via ARD.  
- **Flexibility:** It isn't restricted to continuous data; you can swap the likelihood ‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬‭‬ to handle categorical or count data. 
- **Generative Power:** Unlike regular PCA (which just projects data), PPCA can **generate new data points**. You simply sample a point in the latent space and project it back into the high-dimensional space with added noise.

### PPCA with non-linear projections
Replace the relationship between $\mathbf{z}$ and $\mathbf{x}$ with a non-linear relationship
$$
p(\mathbf{x}_n|\mathbf{z}_n)=\mathcal{N}(\mathbf{x}_n|f_{\theta}(\mathbf{z}_n), \mathbf{\sigma^{2}\mathbf{I}})
$$
where $f_\theta$ is a non-linear function parametrized by $\theta$.

Use Variational Inference to solve.

## Variational Autoencoder (VAE)
1. Pass observations $\mathbf{x}_n$ through $g\to$ the **encoder** network
2. Sample $\mathbf{z}_n\sim \mathcal{N}(\mathbf{z}_n|g_\phi(\mathbf{x}_n))$
3. Pass samples $\mathbf{z}_n^{(s)}$ through $f\to$ the **decoder** network
4. Compute ELBO (loss function) and take gradient step $\to$ backwards pass
