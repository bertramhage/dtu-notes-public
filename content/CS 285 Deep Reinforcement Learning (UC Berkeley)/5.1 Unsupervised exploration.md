>[!info] Prerequisite identities
>$p(\mathbf{x})$ – distribution over observations $\mathbf{x}$
>$\mathcal{H}(p(\mathbf{x}))=-\mathbf{E}_{x\sim p(\mathbf{x})}[\log p(\mathbf{x})]$ – entropy ("broadness of $p$")
>$\mathcal{I}(\mathbf{x};\mathbf{y})=D_\text{KL}(p(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})\mid\mid p(\mathbf{x})p(\mathbf{y}))=\mathbf{E}_{(\mathbf{x,y})\sim p(\mathbf{x,y})}\left[ \log \frac{p(\mathbf{x,y})}{p(\mathbf{x})p(\mathbf{y})} \right]$
>$=\mathcal{H}(p(\mathbf{y}))-\mathcal{H}(p(\mathbf{y}|\mathbf{x}))$ – mutual information

Let
- $\pi(\mathbf{s})$ state marginal distribution of policy $\pi$
- $\mathcal{H}(\pi(\mathbf{s}))$ state marginal entropy of policy $\pi$
- $\mathcal{I}(\mathbf{s}_{t+1};\mathbf{a}_t)=\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{s}_{t+1})-\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{s}_{t+1}|\mathbf{a}_t)$ (empowerment)

Problem: Model as-is will propose goals close to already observed data. 

To diversify goals use a weighted MLE:
$$
\text{weighted MLE}: \theta,\phi \leftarrow \operatorname*{arg\,max}_{\theta,\phi}\mathbf{E}[w(\bar{x})\log p(\bar{x})]
$$
where $w(\bar{x})=p_\theta(\bar{x})^\alpha$.
For any $\alpha \in[-1,0[$, entropy $\mathcal{H}(p_\theta(x))$ increases.

## Objective of unsupervised exploration
The objective is to maximize mutual information between the states $S$ and goals $G$:
$$
\max\mathcal{H}(p(G))-\mathcal{H}(p(G|S)) = \max \mathcal{I}(S;G)
$$
Intuition:
- Good exploration means high $\mathcal{H}(p(G))$
- Effective goal reaching means low $\mathcal{H}(p(G|S))$
