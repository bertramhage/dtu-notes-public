Motivation:
![[Pasted image 20260223092921.png|250]]
Calculate $\hat{Q}_{i,t}$ such that it reflects the true expected reward-to-go.
Thus, the gradient will become
$$
\nabla_\theta J(\theta)\approx \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} \sum_{t=1}^{T} \nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(\mathbf{a}_{i,t}|\mathbf{s}_{i,t})(Q(\mathbf{s}_{i,t}, \mathbf{a}_{i,t})-V(\mathbf{s}_{i,t})), \,  \, 
$$
where
$$
V(\mathbf{s}_t)=\mathbf{E}_{\mathbf{a}_t\sim \pi_\theta(\mathbf{a}_{t}|\mathbf{s}_t)}[Q(\mathbf{s}_t,\mathbf{a}_t)]
$$
is the *value-function* that reflects the expected reward-to-go starting from $\mathbf{s}_t$.

Denote $A^\pi (\mathbf{s}_t,\mathbf{a}_t)=Q^\pi (\mathbf{s}_{i,t}, \mathbf{a}_{i,t})-V^\pi(\mathbf{s}_{i,t})$ the *advantage* function.

We can rewrite
$$
Q^\pi(\mathbf{s}_t,\mathbf{a}_t)=r(\mathbf{s}_t,\mathbf{a}_t)+\underbrace{ \sum_{t'=t+1}^{T} \mathbf{E}_{\pi_\theta}[r(\mathbf{s}_t', \mathbf{a}_t')|\mathbf{s}_t, \mathbf{a}_t] }_{ V^\pi(\mathbf{s}_{t+1}) } \, 
$$
$$
\implies A^\pi(\mathbf{s}_t,\mathbf{a}_t)\approx r(\mathbf{s}_t,\mathbf{a}_t)+V^\pi(\mathbf{s}_{t+1})-V^\pi(\mathbf{s}_t)
$$

## Policy evaluation
Goal: Fit $V^\pi(\mathbf{s})$.

### Monte Carlo evaluation
Policy gradient does
$$
\begin{aligned}
V^\pi(\mathbf{s}_t)&\approx \sum_{t'=t}^{T} r(\mathbf{s}_{t'},\mathbf{a}_{t'}) \text{ or } \\
V^\pi(\mathbf{s}_t)&\approx \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}  \,  \sum_{t'=t}^{T} r(\mathbf{s}_{t'},\mathbf{a}_{t'}) \text{ (better, lower variance)} \, 
\end{aligned}
$$

Alternative: fit a neural network to better estimate $V^\pi$ with 
- traning data: $\left\{  \left( \mathbf{s}_{i,t},\underbrace{ \sum_{t'=t}^{T} r(\mathbf{s}_{i,t'},\mathbf{a}_{i,t'}) }_{ y_{i,t} } \, \right)  \right\}$
- loss: $\mathcal{L}(\phi)=\frac{1}{2}\sum_i\mid\mid \hat{V}_\phi^\pi(\mathbf{s}_i)-y_i\mid\mid^{2}$

or better alternative:
- training data: $\left\{ \left( \mathbf{s}_{i,t},\underbrace{ r(\mathbf{s}_{i,t},\mathbf{a}_{i,t})+\hat{V}\phi^\pi(\mathbf{s}_{i,t+1}) }_{ y_{i,t} } \right) \right\}$
where $\hat{V}_{\phi}^\pi$ is the previous fitted value function

## Online actor-critic
Discount factors with critic:
$$
\nabla_\theta J(\theta)\approx \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} \sum_{t=1}^{T} \nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(\mathbf{a}_{i,t}, \mathbf{s}_{i,t})(r(\mathbf{s}_{i,t}, \mathbf{a}_{i,t}0)+\gamma \hat{V}_{\phi}^\pi(\mathbf{s}_{i,t+1})-\hat{v}_\phi^\pi(\mathbf{s}_{i,t})) \,  \, 
$$
With discounts we can make an online actor-critic algorithm

## Actor-critic design decision

### Network designs
**Two-network design**:
1. Network to map $\mathbf{s}$ to $\hat{V}_\phi^\pi(\mathbf{s})$
2. Network to mak $\mathbf{s}$ to $\pi_\theta(\mathbf{a|s})$

**Shared network design**:
1. Network to map $\mathbf{s}$ to both $\pi_\theta(\mathbf{a|s})$ and $\hat{V}\phi^\pi(\mathbf{s})$
- Shared weights, more difficult to train

## Critics as baselines
Problem: If the critic is not perfect the actor-critic policy is not unbiased.

Use $\hat{V}_\phi^\pi$ as the baseline (unbiased, lower variance than plain policy gradients):
$$
\nabla_\theta J(\theta)\approx \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} \sum_{t=1}^{T}\nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(\mathbf{a}_{i,t}|\mathbf{s}_{i,t})\left( \left( \sum_{t'=t}^{T} \gamma ^{t'-t}r(\mathbf{s}_{i,t'},\mathbf{a}_{i,t'}) \,  \right) -\hat{V}_\phi^\pi(\mathbf{s}_{i,t}) \right)  \,  \, 
$$

Alternative: Make the baseline also depend on the action (lower variance):
$$
\begin{aligned}
\nabla_\theta J(\theta)\approx &\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\sum_{t=1}^{T} \nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(\mathbf{a}_{i,t}|\mathbf{s}_{i,t})(\hat{Q}_{i,t}-Q^\pi_\phi(\mathbf{s}_{i,t}, \mathbf{a}_{i,t})) \\
&+\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N} \sum_{t=1}^{T} \nabla_\theta \mathbf{E}_{\mathbf{a}\sim \pi_\theta(\mathbf{a}_t|\mathbf{s}_{i,t})}[Q^\pi_\phi(\mathbf{s}_{i,t}, \mathbf{a}_t)] \, 
\end{aligned} \, 
$$

## Further steps to manage bias-variance tradeoff

### N-step returns
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\hat{A}_C^\pi(\mathbf{s}_t,\mathbf{a}_t)=r(\mathbf{s}_t,\mathbf{a}_t)+\gamma \hat{V}_{\phi}^\pi(\mathbf{s}_{t+1})-\hat{V}_\phi^\pi(\mathbf{s}_t) \\
&\hat{A}_{MC}^\pi(\mathbf{s}_t,\mathbf{a}_t) = \sum_{t'=t}^{\infty} \gamma ^{t'-t}r(\mathbf{s}_{t'},\mathbf{a}_{t'})-\hat{V}_\phi^\pi(\mathbf{s}_t) \, 
\end{aligned}
$$
Intuition: Predictability is higher in the near future and lower in the distant future. 
$$
\hat{A}_n^\pi(\mathbf{s}_t,\mathbf{a}_t)=\sum_{t'=t}^{t+n} \gamma ^{t'-t}r(\mathbf{s}_{t'},\mathbf{a}_{t'})-\hat{V}_{\phi}^\pi(\mathbf{s}_t)+\gamma^n\hat{V}_\phi^\pi(\mathbf{s}_{t+n})\, 
$$
- Larger $N$: Lower bias, higher variance
- Smaller $N$: Higher bias, lower variance

### Generalised Advantage Estimation (GAE)
Rather than picking a single $N$ **GAE** takes a weighted average of _all_ possible $N$-step returns.
$$
\hat{A}_{GAE}^\pi(\mathbf{s}_t,\mathbf{a}_t)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} w_n\hat{A}_n^\pi (\mathbf{s}_t,\mathbf{a}_t) \, 
$$
$w_n \propto \lambda ^{n-1}$ (exponential falloff)
