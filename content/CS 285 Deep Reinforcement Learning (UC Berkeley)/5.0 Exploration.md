Two potential defintion of exploration problem
- How can an agent discover high-reward strategies that require a temporally extended sequence of complex behaviors that, individually, are not rewarding?
- How can an agent decide wether to attempt new behaviors (to discover ones with higher rewards) or continue to do the best things it knows so far?

 
>[!info] Bandits
>A decision-making scenario where an agent must repeatedly choose between multiple options (arms) with unknown rewards
>Assume $r(a_i)\sim p_{\theta_i}(r_i)$
>eg., $p(r_i=1)=\theta_i$ and $p(r_i=0)=1-\theta_i$
>$\theta_i\sim p(\theta)$ but otherwise unknown
>
>This defines a POMDP with $s=[\theta_1,\dots,\theta_n]$
>Belief state is $\hat{p}(\theta_1,\dots,\theta_n)$
>
>Solving the POMDP yields the optimal exploration strategy.
>
>Regret: Difference from optimal policy at time step $T$:
>$$
>\text{Reg}(T)=TE[r(a^*)]-\sum_{t=1}^{T} r(a_t) \, 
>$$

## Optimistic exploration
Keep track of average reward $\hat{\mu}_a$ for each action $a$
- Pure exploitation: pick $a=\operatorname*{arg\,max}\hat{\mu}_a$
- Optimistic estimate: $a=\operatorname*{arg\,max}\hat{\mu}_a + C\sigma_a$, where $\sigma$ is some sort of variance estimate
- Upper Confidence Bound: $a=\operatorname*{arg\,max}\hat{\mu}_a+\sqrt{ \frac{2\ln T}{N(a)} }$

### Optimistic exploration in Deep RL
Use $r^+(\mathbf{s},\mathbf{a})=r(\mathbf{s,a})+\mathcal{B}(N(\mathbf{s}))$, where $\mathcal{B}$ is a bonus that decreases with $N(\mathbf{s})$, instead of $r(\mathbf{s,a})$ for any model-free algorithm.

In practice for large/infinite state-spaces:
Let $p_\theta(\mathbf{s})$ be a density model estimating a "pseudo-count" $\hat{N}(\mathbf{s})$.
- Fit model $p_\theta(\mathbf{s})$ to all states $\mathcal{D}$ seen so far
- Take step $i$ and observe $\mathbf{s}_i$
- Fit new model $p_{\theta'}$ to $\mathcal{D}\cup \mathbf{s}_i$
- Use $p_\theta(\mathbf{s}_i) \text{ and } p_{\theta}(\mathbf{s}_i)$ to estimate $\hat{N}(\mathbf{s})$
- Set $r^+_i=r_i+\mathcal{B}(\hat{N}(\mathbf{s}))$
- repeat...

$$
\hat{N}(\mathbf{s}_i)=\hat{n}p_\theta(\mathbf{s}_i)\quad \hat{n}=\frac{1-p_{\theta'}(\mathbf{s}_i)}{p_{\theta'}(\mathbf{s}_i)-p_\theta(\mathbf{s}_i)}p_\theta(\mathbf{s}_i)
$$

**Choices of bonuses**:
- UCB: $\mathcal{B}(N(\mathbf{s}))=\sqrt{ \frac{2\ln n}{N(\mathbf{s})} }$
- MBIE-EB: $\mathcal{B}(N(\mathbf{s}))=\sqrt{ \frac{1}{N(\mathbf{s})} }$
- BEB: $\mathcal{B}(N(\mathbf{s}))=\frac{1}{N(\mathbf{s})}$
## Posterior sampling/Thompson sampling:
For a POMDP with $\mathbf{s}=[\theta_1,\dots,\theta_n]$ (ie. a *model* of the bandit).
- sample $\theta_1,\dots,\theta_n\sim \hat{p}(\theta_1,\dots,\theta_n)$
- pretend the model $\theta_1,\dots,\theta_n$ is correct
- take the optimal action
- update the model
- repeat...
$a=\operatorname*{arg\,max}_a\mathbf{E}_{\theta_a}[r(a)]$

### Posterior sampling in Deep RL
MDP analog is the $Q$-function.
- Sample $Q$-function $Q$ from $p(Q)$
- Act according to $Q$ for one episode
- Update $p(Q)$
- repeat...

Get $p(Q)$ sampling from bootstrapped models $f_{\theta_{i}}$ where $i \in [1,\dots,N]$ where $N$ is models trained on bootstrapped datasets $\mathcal{D}_i$ or (more efficiently) a shared network with $N$ heads.


## Information gain
For a latent variable $z$ and observed variable $y$
$$
\text{IG}(z,y)=\mathbf{E}_y[\mathcal{H}(\hat{p}(z))-\mathcal{H}(\hat{p}(z)|y)]
$$
where
- $\mathcal{H}(\hat{p}(z))$ is the current entropy of our $z$ estimate
- $\mathcal{H}(\hat{p}(z|y))$ is the entropy of our $z$ estimate after observation $y$ (eg., $y$ might be $r(a)$)
- 
**Example**:
$y=r_a$, $z=\theta_a$ (parameters of model $p(r_a)$)
$g(a)=\text{IG}(\theta_a,r_a|a)$ - information gain of $a$
$\Delta(a)=\mathbf{E}[r(a^*)-r(a)]$ – expected sub-optimality of $a$

choose $a$ according to $\operatorname*{arg\,min}_a \frac{\Delta(a)^{2}}{g(a)}$

### Information gain in Deep RL
Estimate
- IG about reward
- IG about state density $p(\mathbf{s})$
- IG about dynamics $p(\mathbf{s}'|\mathbf{s},\mathbf{a})$

Generally intractable to use exactly, regardless of what is being estimated:

#### Prediction gain
$$
\log p_{\theta}(\mathbf{s})-\log p_\theta(\mathbf{s})
$$
#### Variational inference
Express IG as 
$$
D_{\text{KL}}(p(\theta|h,y)\mid\mid p(\theta|h))$$where $h$ is the history and $y=(s_t,a_t,s_{t+1})$, and $s_{t+1}\sim p_\theta(s_{t+1}|s_t,a_t)$.

**VIVE implementation:**
Optimize variational lower bound
$$
D_{\text{KL}}(q(\theta|\phi)\mid\mid p(h|\theta)p(\theta))
$$
where $q(\theta|\phi)\approx p(\theta|h)$ is a product of independent Gaussians with mean $\phi$.

Given new transition $(s,a,s')$ update $\phi$ to get $\phi'$. Use $D_{\text{KL}}(q(\theta|\phi')\mid\mid q(\theta|\phi))$ as approximate bonus.

## Unsupervised exploration

>[!info] Prerequisite identities
>$p(\mathbf{x})$ – distribution over observations $\mathbf{x}$
>$\mathcal{H}(p(\mathbf{x}))=-\mathbf{E}_{x\sim p(\mathbf{x})}[\log p(\mathbf{x})]$ – entropy ("broadness of $p$")
>$\mathcal{I}(\mathbf{x};\mathbf{y})=D_\text{KL}(p(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{y})\mid\mid p(\mathbf{x})p(\mathbf{y}))=\mathbf{E}_{(\mathbf{x,y})\sim p(\mathbf{x,y})}\left[ \log \frac{p(\mathbf{x,y})}{p(\mathbf{x})p(\mathbf{y})} \right]$
>$=\mathcal{H}(p(\mathbf{y}))-\mathcal{H}(p(\mathbf{y}|\mathbf{x}))$ – mutual information

Let
- $\pi(\mathbf{s})$ state marginal distribution of policy $\pi$
- $\mathcal{H}(\pi(\mathbf{s}))$ state marginal entropy of policy $\pi$
- $\mathcal{I}(\mathbf{s}_{t+1};\mathbf{a}_t)=\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{s}_{t+1})-\mathcal{H}(\mathbf{s}_{t+1}|\mathbf{a}_t)$ (empowerment)
