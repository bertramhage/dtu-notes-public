In many practical problems, transition dynamics are already known or easily obtainable:
- **Known rules / simulators:** Chess, Go, Atari games, or simulated environments.
- **Modeling:** Some physical systems can be easily approximated with equations of motion (e.g. car driving).
- **System identification:** When dynamics aren't known, we can fit the unknown parameters of a known model.

## Deterministic planning
Select a sequence of actions to minimize total costs given known transition dynamics
$$\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{\mathbf{a}_1, \dots, \mathbf{a}_T} \sum_{t=1}^T c(\mathbf{s}_t, \mathbf{a}_t) \text{ s.t. }\mathbf{s}_{t+1} = f(\mathbf{s}_t, \mathbf{a}_t)$$

## Stochastic planning
**Open-loop-case**: Actions are pre-comitted
$$p(\mathbf{s}_1, \dots, \mathbf{s}_T \mid \mathbf{a}_1, \dots, \mathbf{a}_T) = p(\mathbf{s}_1) \prod_{t=1}^{T-1} p(\mathbf{s}_{t+1} \mid \mathbf{s}_t, \mathbf{a}_t)$$
$$\operatorname*{arg\,max}_{\mathbf{a}_1, \dots, \mathbf{a}_T}\quad  \mathbf{E} \left[ \sum_{t=1}^T r(\mathbf{s}_t, \mathbf{a}_t) \mid \mathbf{a}_1, \dots, \mathbf{a}_T \right]$$

**Closed-loop-case**: Policy-based
$$p(\mathbf{s}_1, \dots, \mathbf{s}_T \mid \mathbf{a}_1, \dots, \mathbf{a}_T) = p(\mathbf{s}_1) \prod_{t=1}^{T-1} \pi(\mathbf{a}_t,\mathbf{s}_t)p(\mathbf{s}_{t+1} \mid \mathbf{s}_t, \mathbf{a}_t)$$
$$
\pi=\operatorname*{arg\,max}_\pi\quad \mathbf{E}_{\tau \sim p(\tau)}\left[ \sum_{t}^r(\mathbf{s}_t,\mathbf{a}_t)  \,  \right] 
$$
## Stochastic optimisation
For open-loop planning.

### Random shooting method
1. Pick $\mathbf{A}_{1},\dots,\mathbf{A}_N$ from some distribution (eg. uniform)
2. Choose $\mathbf{A}_i$ based on $\operatorname*{arg\,max}_i J(\mathbf{A})$, where $\mathbf{a}_{1},\dots,\mathbf{a}_T=\operatorname*{arg\,max}_{\mathbf{a}_1,\dots,\mathbf{a}_T} J(\mathbf{a}_{1},\dots,\mathbf{a}_T)$

### Cross entropy method (CEM)
1. Sample $\mathbf{A}_{1},\dots,\mathbf{A}_N$ from $p(\mathbf{A})$ (initialising $p(\cdot)$ as eg uniform)
2. Evaluate $J(\mathbf{A}_{1}),\dots,J(\mathbf{A}_N)$
3. Pick *elites* $\mathbf{A}_{i_1},\dots,\mathbf{A}_{i_M}$ with highest value, $M<N$
4. Refit $p(\mathbf{A})$ to the elites $\mathbf{A}_{i_1},\dots,\mathbf{A}_{i_M}$

### Monte Carlo Tree Search (MCTS)
1. Find a leaf $s_l$ using TreePolicy($s_1$)
2. Evaluate the load using DefaultPolicy($s_l$)
3. Update all values in tree between $s_1$ and $s_l$
Take best action from $s_l$

**UCT TreePolicy($s_t$)**:
- if $s_t$ not fully expanded, choose new $a_t$
- else choose child with best Score($s_{t+1}$)
$\text{Score}(s_t)=\frac{Q(s_t)}{N(s_t)}+2C\sqrt{  \frac{2\ln N(s_{t-1})}{N(s_t)} }$

## Using derivatives (LQR)
Consider optimizing the objective
$$
\min_{\mathbf{u}_{1},\dots,\mathbf{u}_T}\sum_{t=1}^{T} c(\mathbf{x}_t,\mathbf{u}_t) \text{ s.t. } \mathbf{x}_t=f(\mathbf{x}_{t-1},\mathbf{u}_{t-1}) \, 
$$
equivalent to
$$
\min_{\mathbf{u}_{1},\dots,\mathbf{u}_T}c(\mathbf{x}_{1},\mathbf{u}_{1})+c(f(\mathbf{x}_{1},\mathbf{u}_{1}), \mathbf{u}_{2})+\cdots+c(f(f(\dots)\dots),\mathbf{u}_T)
$$
### Linear LQR
For
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(\mathbf{x}_t, \mathbf{u}_t)&=\mathbf{F}_t\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{x}_t \\\mathbf{u_t}\end{bmatrix}+\mathbf{f_t} \\
c(\mathbf{x}_t,\mathbf{u}_t)&=\frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{x}_t\\\mathbf{u}_t\end{bmatrix}^\top\mathbf{C}_t\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{x}_t\\\mathbf{u}_t\end{bmatrix}+\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{x}_t\\\mathbf{u}_t\end{bmatrix}^\top\mathbf{c}_t
\end{aligned}
$$
such that $f$ is linear and $c$ is quadratic the *backward* recursion starts from the last step $t=T$ and moves backward to $t=1$.

For $t=T$ to 1:
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{Q}_t &= \mathbf{C}_t + \mathbf{F}_t^T \mathbf{V}_{t+1} \mathbf{F}_t \\
\mathbf{q}_t &= \mathbf{c} + \mathbf{F}_t^T \mathbf{V}_{t+1} \mathbf{f}_t + \mathbf{F}_t^T \mathbf{v}_{t+1} \\
Q(\mathbf{x}_t, \mathbf{u}_t) &= \text{const} + \frac{1}{2} \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{x}_t \\ \mathbf{u}_t \end{bmatrix}^T \mathbf{Q}_t \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{x}_t \\ \mathbf{u}_t \end{bmatrix} + \begin{bmatrix} \mathbf{x}_t \\ \mathbf{u}_t \end{bmatrix}^T \mathbf{q}_t \\
\mathbf{u}_t &\leftarrow \arg\min_{\mathbf{u}_t} Q(\mathbf{x}_t, \mathbf{u}_t) = \mathbf{K}_t \mathbf{x}_t + \mathbf{k}_t \\
\mathbf{K}_t &= -\mathbf{Q}_{\mathbf{u}_t, \mathbf{u}_t}^{-1} \mathbf{Q}_{\mathbf{u}_t, \mathbf{x}_t} \\
\mathbf{k}_t &= -\mathbf{Q}_{\mathbf{u}_t, \mathbf{u}_t}^{-1} \mathbf{q}_{\mathbf{u}_t} \\
\mathbf{V}_t &= \mathbf{Q}_{\mathbf{x}_t, \mathbf{x}_t} + \mathbf{Q}_{\mathbf{x}_t, \mathbf{u}_t} \mathbf{K}_t + \mathbf{K}_t^T \mathbf{Q}_{\mathbf{u}_t, \mathbf{x}_t} + \mathbf{K}_t^T \mathbf{Q}_{\mathbf{u}_t, \mathbf{u}_t} \mathbf{K}_t \\
\mathbf{v}_t &= \mathbf{q}_{\mathbf{x}_t} + \mathbf{Q}_{\mathbf{x}_t, \mathbf{u}_t} \mathbf{k}_t + \mathbf{K}_t^T \mathbf{Q}_{\mathbf{u}_t} + \mathbf{K}_t^T \mathbf{Q}_{\mathbf{u}_t, \mathbf{u}_t} \mathbf{k}_t \\
V(\mathbf{x}_t) &= \text{const} + \frac{1}{2} \mathbf{x}_t^T \mathbf{V}_t \mathbf{x}_t + \mathbf{x}_t^T \mathbf{v}_t
\end{aligned}
$$

Once at $t=1$ we know $x_1$ and simulate *forward* to $T$.

For $t=1$ to $T$
$$
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{u}_t &=\mathbf{K}_t\mathbf{x}_t+\mathbf{k}_t \\
\mathbf{x}_{t+1}&=f(\mathbf{x}_t,\mathbf{u}_t)
\end{aligned}
$$

### Stochastic dynamics (LQGR)
For transition dynamics
$$
\mathbf{x}_{t+1}\sim p(\mathbf{x}_{t+1}|\mathbf{x}_t,\mathbf{u}_t)
$$
where
$$
p(\mathbf{x}_{t+1}|\mathbf{x}_t,\mathbf{u}_t)=\mathcal{N}\left( \mathbf{F}_t\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{x}_t\\\mathbf{u}_t\end{bmatrix}+\mathbf{f}_t, \Sigma_t\right) 
$$
$\mathbf{x}_t\sim p(\mathbf{x}_t)$ is no longer deterministic, but $p(\mathbf{x}_t)$ is Gaussian.

No change to algorithm. We can ignore $\Sigma_t$ due to symmetry of Gaussians.

### Non-linear case (iLQR)
For non-linear $f$ and/or non-quadratic $c$ we apply the Taylor-expansion
$$
\begin{aligned}
f(\mathbf{x}_t,\mathbf{u}_t)&\approx f(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t, \hat{\mathbf{u}}_t)+\nabla_{\mathbf{x}_t,\mathbf{u}_t}f(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t,\hat{\mathbf{u}}_t)\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{x}_t-\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t\\\hat{\mathbf{u}}_t-\hat{\mathbf{u}}_t\end{bmatrix} \\
c(\mathbf{x}_t,\mathbf{u}_t)&\approx c(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t,\hat{\mathbf{u}}_t)+\nabla_{\mathbf{x}_t,\mathbf{u}_t}c(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t,\hat{\mathbf{u}}_t)\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{x}_t-\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t\\ \mathbf{u}_t-\hat{\mathbf{u}}_t\end{bmatrix} \\
&+\frac{1}{2}\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{x}_t-\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t\\\mathbf{u}_t-\hat{\mathbf{u}}_t\end{bmatrix}^\top\nabla^{2}_{\mathbf{x}_t,\mathbf{u}_t}c(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t, \hat{\mathbf{u}}_t)\begin{bmatrix}\mathbf{x}_t-\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t\\\mathbf{u}_t-\hat{\mathbf{u}}_t\end{bmatrix}
\end{aligned}
$$
**Iterative LQR**:
Until convergence:
$$
\begin{aligned}
&\mathbf{F}_t=\nabla_{\mathbf{x}_t,\mathbf{u}_t}f(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t, \hat{\mathbf{u}}_t) \\
&\mathbf{c}_t=\nabla_{\mathbf{x}_t, \mathbf{u}_t}c(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t,\hat{\mathbf{u}}_t) \\
&\mathbf{C}_t=\nabla^{2}_{\mathbf{x}_t,\mathbf{u}_t}c(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t,\hat{\mathbf{u}}_t) \\
&\text{Run}\text{ LQR backward pass on state }\delta \mathbf{x_t}=\mathbf{x}_t-\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t \text{ and } \text{action }\delta \mathbf{u}_t=\mathbf{u}_t-\hat{\mathbf{u}}_t \\
&\text{Run} \text{ forward pass with real nonlinear dynamics and }\mathbf{u_t}=\mathbf{K}_t(\mathbf{x}_t-\hat{\mathbf{x}}_t)+\mathbf{k}_t+\hat{\mathbf{u}}_t \\
&\text{Update } \hat{\mathbf{x}}_t \text{ and } \hat{\mathbf{u}}_t \text{ based on states and actions in forward pass}
\end{aligned}
$$
