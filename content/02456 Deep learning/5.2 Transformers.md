## Attention
Intuition: Let surrounding tokens influence the meaning of a given token

![[Pasted image 20251006132810.png]]

In an attention layer we input
- A *query* vector $\mathbf{q} \in \mathbb{R}^{1\times D}$
- A *key* sequence $K \in \mathbb{R}^{T'\times D}$
- A *value* sequence $V \in \mathbb{R}^{T'\times D'}$

Attention:
$$
e_t'=\mathbf{q}\cdot \mathbf{K}_{t'} \text{ or } \mathbf{e}=\mathbf{q}K^T
$$
$$
\mathbf{a}=\text{softmax}(\mathbf{q}K^{T})
$$
The output $\mathbf{y} \in\mathbb{R}^{D'}$ is a weighing of $\mathbf{V}_{t}$
$$
\mathbf{y}=\sum_{t'}a_{t'}V_{t'}=\mathbf{a}V  \, 
$$
### Scaled dot-product attention
$$
A=\text{softmax}_\text{row}\left( \frac{QK^{T}}{\sqrt{ D }} \right) 
$$
$$
Y=AV
$$

### Multi-head attention
$$
\text{MultiHead}(Q,K,V)=\text{Concat}(\text{head}_1,\dots,\text{head}_h)W^O
$$
where
$$
\text{head}_i=\text{Attention}(QW_i^Q, KW_i^K,VW_i^V)
$$
$$
\text{Attention}(Q,K,V)=\text{softmax}\left( \frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{ d_k }} \right) V
$$
## Self-attention
In self-attention 
$$Q=K=V$$
- Connects all positions directly $\to$learns long-range dependencies
- High parallelism
- Interpretability
## Transformer layer
A transoformer layer consists of
- Multi-head self-attention
- Layer normalization
- Position-wise FFN
- Residual connections
